Who Are We

A Premier Gateway to Professional Growth

Admission FAQs
Got Questions?
How do I apply for admission?
You can initiate registration in person by visiting our Peshawar Hashtnagri campus, or via the online form inside contact.html. Walk into our counselor office with copies of your latest certificates to select your batches.
When does the next batch start?
New cohorts commence twice annually during Spring and Autumn semesters. Admissions are currently open for current sessions, and classes are filled in linear order.
What documents are required for enrollment?
You need to bring photocopies of your matriculation or secondary high school mark transcripts, your CNIC or B-Form, 3 passport-sized background photos, and completed registration fees.
How many classes are there per week?
The general stream holds classes 4 to 5 days weekly. Our English speed classes run on intensive alternating schedules to provide flexible morning or evening combinations.
Are certificates government recognized?
Yes, all clinical Allied Health programs and IT diplomas are governed under recognized state licensing bureaus and fully support valid registration processes.
Is there a fee installment option?
To make premium training accessible, our accounts desk offers flexible partial installments covering monthly partitions. Consult with accounts during registration to select your layout.

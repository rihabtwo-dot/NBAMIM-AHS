Our Story

Who We Are

New Bab-Al-Madina Institute of Management and Allied Health Sciences (NBAMIM & AHS) was established in 2020 with a vision to provide quality, career-focused education in Peshawar. Located at Hashtnagri BRT Station, our institute is dedicated to equipping students with practical skills in Allied Health Sciences, English Language, and Computer & IT programs.

We believe that education is not just about degrees — it is about developing capable, confident, and career-ready individuals. Our experienced faculty, modern facilities, and student-centered approach make us the premier choice for professional education in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Our Responsibility

Giving Back to the Community

New Bab-Al-Madina Institute of Management and Allied Health Sciences (NBAMIM & AHS) strongly believes inside structural civic duty. We are proud to offer deep merit-based scholarship campaigns design to lower financial burdens for deserving regional talents and families.

We regularly run free public health awareness camps, blood-screening tests, first-aid instruction workshops, and digital competence seminars throughout Hashtnagri and greater Peshawar, securing a healthier, enlightened local community.
